Population Growth

Currently home to over 170,000 residents, Oshawa's population is forecasted to grow to over 190,000 by 2031. Employment is expected to rise from 80,000 to 90,000 jobs in the same period.

Housing Needs

Durham Region will need over 89,000 new homes by 2031, but can only deliver an estimated 65,000. This shortfall will increase demand and put upward pressure on real estate values and rents.

Education

Oshawa is home to three post-secondary institutions: Durham College, Ontario Tech University, and Trent University, drawing tens of thousands of students annually.
